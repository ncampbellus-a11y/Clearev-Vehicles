Privacy Policy

How we collect, use, and protect your information.

Who we are

Clearev Consultants ("we," "us") is a vehicle sourcing brokerage operating nationwide. We respect your privacy and want you to know exactly what information we collect and how it's used.

Information we collect

When you submit our intake form or book a consultation, we collect your name, phone number, email address, and vehicle preferences. This information is collected through Tally (our form provider) and Cal.com (our scheduling provider).

How we use it

We use this information to source vehicles on your behalf, communicate with you about your search and purchase, and process your transaction.

Who we share it with

We do not share your information with dealers, auction houses, or any other third party. Tally and Cal.com act as our form and scheduling providers and process data under their own privacy policies, available at tally.so/privacy and cal.com/privacy.

Payment information

All payments are made by wire transfer. We do not collect or store credit card or bank account numbers.

Contact us

Questions about this policy can be sent to info@clearevconsultants.com.
