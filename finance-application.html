Finance Your Vehicle

Get financing in place before we start sourcing and bidding on your vehicle.

How it works

Clearev offers financing for qualified buyers. To get started, we'll send you a credit application to complete, along with a copy of your driver's license and insurance card. Once that's in place, we begin sourcing and bidding on your vehicle with financing already lined up.

What you'll need

  • A completed credit application (we'll send this to you directly)
  • A valid driver's license
  • A current insurance card
